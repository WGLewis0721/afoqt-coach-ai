## Mechanics
- Newton 1: body at rest/constant velocity stays that way unless net force.
- Newton 2: F = ma. Force in newtons, mass kg, accel m/s².
- Newton 3: equal and opposite forces.
- Weight W = mg. g ≈ 9.8 m/s² ≈ 32 ft/s².
- Work W = F d cosθ. Energy in joules.
- KE = (1/2) m v²
- PE gravitational = m g h
- Power P = W/t = F v
- Momentum p = m v. Conserved in isolated collisions.
- Impulse = Δp = F Δt

Uniform accel:
- v = v0 + a t
- d = v0 t + (1/2) a t²
- v² = v0² + 2 a d

Friction: kinetic f = μk N; static up to μs N.
Circular: centripetal a = v²/r, F = m v²/r toward center.

## Simple machines
Lever, pulley, wheel-axle, inclined plane, wedge, screw.
Mechanical advantage ≈ load / effort (ideal).
Inclined plane: effort component mg sinθ.

## Waves and sound
v = f λ
Sound needs a medium. Fastest in solids, slower in liquids, slowest in gases. Vacuum: no sound.
Frequency → pitch. Amplitude → loudness.
Doppler: source toward you → higher frequency.

## Light
c = 3 × 10^8 m/s in vacuum.
Reflection: angle in = angle out.
Refraction: bends toward normal when entering slower medium (higher n).
n = c / v
Concave/convex lenses: convex (converging) can magnify and focus sunlight; concave diverges.
ROYGBIV: red longest visible wavelength, violet shortest.
Ultraviolet higher energy than violet; infrared lower energy than red.

## Electricity
Charge: proton +, electron −, neutron 0.
Current I = Q/t (amperes)
Ohm: V = I R
Power P = I V = I² R = V²/R
Series: same current, resistances add.
Parallel: same voltage, 1/Req = 1/R1 + 1/R2
Magnetism: moving charge / current makes a field. Right-hand rule for direction.

## Heat and thermo
Heat flows hot → cold.
Conduction (contact), convection (fluid motion), radiation (EM waves).
Q = m c ΔT (no phase change)
Phase changes: fusion/melting, vaporization, condensation, sublimation, deposition. Temperature stays constant during a pure phase change at a given pressure.
Absolute zero: 0 K = −273 °C.
K = °C + 273
Density = m/V. Water 1 g/cm³. Ice less dense than liquid water.

## Chemistry
Atom: nucleus protons+neutrons, electrons in shells.
Atomic number Z = protons. Mass number = P + N.
Isotopes: same Z, different N.
Periodic table: groups (columns) similar chemistry. Alkali metals highly reactive. Noble gases inert.
Ionic bond: transfer (metal + nonmetal). Covalent: share (nonmetals).
pH: 7 neutral, <7 acid, >7 base. Each unit is 10× H+ concentration.
Balanced equations: atoms conserved.
Oxidation: loss of electrons. Reduction: gain. OIL RIG.
Avogadro / mole: 6.02 × 10^23 particles. Molar mass from periodic table.
States: solid definite shape+volume, liquid definite volume, gas neither.

## Earth / atmosphere (light)
Layers: troposphere (weather) → stratosphere (ozone) → mesosphere → thermosphere.
Greenhouse gases trap outgoing IR.
Seasons from axial tilt, not distance to sun.
Moon phases from geometry of sun-earth-moon.

## Units to not mix
SI: m, kg, s, N, J, W, Pa, A, V, Ω
1 N = 1 kg m/s²
1 J = 1 N·m
1 Pa = 1 N/m²
Standard atmosphere ≈ 101.3 kPa ≈ 14.7 psi ≈ 29.92 inHg
