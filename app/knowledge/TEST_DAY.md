# Test day and study tactics

## Before the test
- Sleep. The battery is long. Fatigue wrecks Table Reading and Instrument Comprehension first.
- Eat protein + slow carbs. Bring allowed water if the site permits it in the holding area, not the room.
- Know the address, parking, ID requirements, and start time. Late = no test.
- No calculator. Analog watch without calculator functions is the usual safe choice. Many eAFOQT rooms display a timer.
- Scratch paper and pencils are provided. Use them for AR, MK, and block counting hash marks.

## During
- Answer every item. No guessing penalty.
- Pace by section, not by "finish the whole test."
- On speed sections (WK, TR, IC, BC) mark and move. A perfect item you never reach is a zero.
- On AR/MK, if an item is still open after one honest attempt, pick and flag mentally, keep moving.
- SJ: most effective + least effective. Do not pick the heroic option that breaks the chain of command or the timid option that hides a safety problem.
- SDI: consistent and honest. Do not try to game a personality inventory. Validity scales exist in this family of instruments.

## Guessing
When two remain, pick one immediately. When you have no idea:
- Verbal: prefer the bland precise word over the dramatic one.
- Math: plug choices from the middle.
- Aviation: prefer the textbook PHAK answer over hangar talk.
- Science: prefer definitions and first-principles units.

## Study calendar (4 weeks, cyber / software)

Week 1 — Quant foundation
- Diagnostic mixed quiz on VA/AR/WK/MK/RC only.
- MK formulas daily (30–45 min). No calculator.
- AR translation drill: one equation per word problem.

Week 2 — Quant + verbal speed
- Keep MK daily.
- WK roots + 20 flashcards/day at 12 seconds/item.
- VA relationship types (bridge sentence).
- One timed AR set.

Week 3 — Academic lock
- RC: 2 timed passages/day.
- Mixed Verbal (VA+WK) sprints.
- Weak MK topics only (the miss log, not the whole book).
- Light PS if leftover time. Not required for 17X.

Week 4 — Dress rehearsals
- Two sessions that chain VA → AR → WK → MK → RC with real clocks.
- Guess through AI/IC/TR/BC on test day. Do not study them this week.
- Light SJ using the core-values filter. Honest SDI.

Daily minimum if short on time (45 min):
1. 20 min MK
2. 10 min AR or WK
3. 10 min VA or RC
4. 5 min miss-log review

Rated spatial work is optional for this track. Use OATTS only if you also want a cockpit.
